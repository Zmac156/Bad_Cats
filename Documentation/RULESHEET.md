 # Skill Shot
At the beginning of every ball or after locking a ball, plunge into the far left lane to be awarded a skill shot.  The skill shot begins at 200k and for every time it’s collected it increases by 100k.  The skillshot value maxes at 900k

 # Multiball
 ## Light Locks
 There are six cooks in the game.  Each cooks has a set of two corresponding switches:
| Cooks    | Switch 1             | Switch 2             |
| -------- | -------------------- | -------------------- |
| BullDog  | L_Left_Standup_Bank  | R_Left_Standup_Bank  |
| Snake    | L_Right_Standup_Bank | R_Right_Standup_Bank |
| Rat      | Right_Standup        | Center_Standup       |
| Pig      | Right_Inlane         | Right_Orbit          |
| Goldfish | Pop_Saucer           | Side_Ramp            |
| Penguin  | L_Top_Lane           | R_Top_Lane           |

At the beginning of the game, one chef will be flashing (Pig).  Complete the corresponding switches to collect the chef and light the first lock under the side ramp.  Once the first lock is collected, another chef will be flashing (Bulldog).  Complete the corresponding switches to collect the chef and light the second lock on the pop saucer. Launching a ball after completing the second lock begins multiball.  For the second multiball, two chefs per phase will be required to light locks (Pig and Goldfish for the first lock, Bulldog and Penguin for the second) and for all multiballs after the second, three chefs will be required per lock (Pig, Goldfish, and Snake for the first lock, Bulldog, Penguin, and Rat for the second).

## Multiball Scoring
At the beginning of multiball, the side ramp will be available for a jackpot.  After collecting the jackpot, the ball will feed back to the upper flipper and the player can quickly shoot under the side ramp for a double jackpot.  The jackpot value begins at 2 million and increases by 500k for each jackpot collected for a maximum of 5 million.  The jackpot value resets at the start of every multiball.

# Orders
## Completing Orders
There are six different orders in the game.  Each order has a set of two corresponding shots:
| Orders | Shot 1         | Shot 2         |
| ------ | -------------- | -------------- |
| 1      | Left_Ramp_Exit | Left_Ramp_Exit |
| 2      | Left_Ramp_Exit | Pop_Saucer     |
| 3      | Left_Ramp_Exit | Right_Saucer   |
| 4      | Pop_Saucer     | Center_Standup |
| 5      | Pop_Saucer     | Side_Ramp      |
| 6      | Side_Ramp      | Center_Standup |
In an order, hitting shot 1 will light shot 2 for a brief period of time.  Hitting shot 2 when lit will begin a hurryup that can be collected on the scoop to complete the order.  Once the two shot combo has been completed, the order is complete. 

## Order Hurryups
The hurryup will count down to zero over the course of 20 seconds.  When an order hurryup is running, completing another order will reset the timer, the hurryup value is reset, and an amount is added to the hurryup value based on the following table:
| Orders | Amount Added to Hurryup Value |
| ------ | ----------------------------- |
| 1      | 100k                          |
| 2      | 200k                          |
| 3      | 300k                          |
| 4      | 400k                          |
| 5      | 500k                          |
| 6      | 1m                            |

## Dinner Rush
Completing the last order lights the scoop for Dinner Rush.  Dinner Rush is a timed switch frenzy.  For 30 seconds, every switch adds 100k to the Dinner Rush value.  Shooting the scoop finishes the mode and adds the Dinner Rush value to the end-of-ball bonus.

# Promotoion
The first time the player completes the 3-bank drop targets, the left end-lane lights for promotion.  Promotion is a 20 second timed feature that can be started at any time and multiplies all playfield values.  While promotion is running, shooting the left end-lane restarts the timer.  The playfield multiplier value is dependent on the number of 3-banks completed before the feature is started.  Each 3-bank completion increases the playfield multiplier by 1x, up to a maximum of 5x.  Once promotion ends, the playfield multiplier is reset back to 1x.
| Mutliplier | Promotoion  |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| 1          | Dishwasher  |
| 2          | Kitchen Boy |
| 3          | Cook        |
| 4          | Sous Chef   |
| 5          | Head Chef   |

# Bonus
## Bonus Value
When the spinner is unlit, every spin increases the base bonus by 1k.  When the spinner is lit, every spin increases the base bonus by 3k.  The base bonus maxes at 500k.  Shooting the side ramp lights the spinner for the next spinner shot.

## Bonus Multiplier
Completing the top lanes increases bonus x up to a maximum of 8x.  If Bonus x is maxed, completing top lanes awards 50k.

# Pop Bumpers
## Pop Value
Pops when unlit score 1k, when lit score 5k, and when flashing score 10k.  Each saucer, when hit, adds a light to one pop (starting from the top pop and moving in clockwise order).

## Pop Jackpot
Every 5 saucers briefly lights the pop standup to collect the pop jackpot.  The pop jackpot is worth the cumulative value of all pops scored that ball.  The pop jackpot is only reset at the start of the ball.

# Kickback
The kickback starts unlit.  Comboing the right inlane into the target to the left_standup will light kickback.  The timer of the combo will begin at 10 seconds, then 5 seconds, and finally 1 second to light all remaining kickbacks.

# Back Alley Deals
Rolling through the left inlane will briefly light the right orbit for back door deals.  The possible values to be collected from back door deals are:
- Light Special (Once Per Game)
- Light Extra Ball (Once Per Game)
- Random small Value (Between 5k and 100k)
- Random Medium Value (Between 101k and 250k)
- Random Big Value (Between 251k and 500k)
- Increase Promotion x (When Not Maxed)
- Light Kickback (When Not Lit)
- Boost Bonus (Boosts by 25k) (When Not Maxed)
- Award Bonus x (When Not Maxed)
- Boost Skill Shot (When Not Maxed or on Ball 3)
- Hold Bonus (Award once per ball)

# Extra Balls
Extra ball is lit either by reaching the 30th left ramp, the 15th pop saucer, or as an option in back alley deals.  Extra Ball is collected on the right orbit.

# Special
Special is light either by reaching the maximum bonus x, or as an option in back alley deals.  Special is collected on the right outlane.
